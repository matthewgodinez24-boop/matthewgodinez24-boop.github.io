ReLease: testing the first outreach

6 warm DMs sent. 3 replies. 2 marked posted. One batch, Jul 14, 2026.

Sole founder and operator · Positioning, outreach and AI-assisted product building.

ReLease helps Berkeley renters research a home before signing: public housing records plus reviews from renters who verify their email. Visit ReLease.

The audience and the ask

A new review marketplace needs useful coverage before renters have a reason to return. I concentrated outreach around a specific property manager, asking renters in my warm network to describe their experience. The first logged batch targeted Premium Properties.

The outreach kit calls for a direct link to the renter’s building or manager. The goal is a relevant review on that page, not an undirected homepage visit.

Inspect the message

What the batch recorded

“Posted” is the tracker’s review-posting status; I have not matched those entries to individual approved reviews. This small historical batch does not establish repeatable acquisition. Original log transcription (TXT).

What changed after the test

I kept distribution focused on renter networks and individual building pages. I also separated product QA and spam from acquisition reporting: a production check prompted a pause to remove test reviews, and a later signup-spam diagnosis led to double opt-in.

Next measurement: retain the sent wording and connect outreach records to approved reviews, so the full path can be checked. No current total-review claim is made here.
