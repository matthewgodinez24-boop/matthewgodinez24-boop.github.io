Reqest | Reimbursement amount-integrity specification excerpt

Historical source: T-001-ch03-amount-integrity.md, acceptance checks AC-1, AC-2 and AC-4; BR-83; September 8, 2026. Inspected from repository main on November 15, 2026.

Problem
Testing found that an approved reimbursement could be recorded as paid at a different amount. The source's illustrative failure was approval for $300 followed by a $500 recorded payment. Those are test amounts, not a real loss.

Decision
An approved reimbursement must preserve a single, receipt-backed amount through approval and payment. A legitimate change requires renewed approval, rather than silently accepting whatever amount the payer enters.

Original acceptance excerpt (AC-4)
“A legitimate amount change is possible through the explicit path and leaves the request back in a state that requires approval, with an audit event carrying the reason (INV-3, BR-43).”

Additional acceptance criteria (condensed)
- A reimbursement cannot move to PAID at a different amount without an explicit re-approval event.
- The former approve-$300/pay-$500 test must assert rejection.
- The separate preapproval flow continues to work.
