A live review marketplace with 27,657 addresses on file, built and launched solo (as of Nov 11, 2026)

What it is and my role

ReLease is a two-sided marketplace where verified renters review their landlords. It is live at joinrelease.com.

I am the sole founder and operator. I built and launched it solo, end to end. Nobody else worked on it.

The problem

A review marketplace launches with zero supply. The incumbent, och.berkeley.edu, is operated by CoStar and cannot carry reviews.

What I did

  1. Ran a competitive teardown of CoStar's och.berkeley.edu and found it cannot carry reviews.
  2. Built and launched the product: an address directory with reviews from renters who verify their email.
  3. Ran one outreach batch to seed reviews.
  4. Audited production read-only instead of trusting my own status document. The audit overturned an earlier all-clear, so I paused seeding until it was fixed.
  5. Diagnosed a 17-signup surge as form spam instead of reporting it as growth, and shipped double opt-in. (My own account as of Sep 19, 2026, not re-measured.)

Results

  • 27,657 addresses and 27 landlords on file (as of Nov 11, 2026).
  • Email verification completion: 12 of 12 (as of Jul 25, 2026).
  • Outreach: 6 sent, 3 replied, 2 posted, from one batch (as of Jul 14, 2026).

What went wrong

ReLease is live, but I under-invested in distribution and over-invested in the build. What I would do differently is set the supply target before the build target, not after.
