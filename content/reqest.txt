## What it is and my role
Reqest handles spending requests, approvals and budgets for student organizations. It is deployed at [reqest.app](https://reqest.app).

I am the founder. I founded Reqest and built the first version solo through {{reqest_solo_through}}. A technical co-founder joined in {{reqest_cofounder_joined}}.

## The problem
I needed to hear from student organizations outside my own network, and to judge what I heard against criteria I could not adjust afterwards.

## What I did
1. Cold-emailed student organizations outside my network.
2. Held {{reqest_calls}} discovery calls: Purdue Space Program and TAMU SRT.
3. Wrote a go-to-market research report with pass/fail criteria registered before the results came in.
4. Re-scored my own failing verdict against primary-source student-government minutes.

The report and the re-score are my own account as of {{reqest_report:date}}, not re-measured.

## Results
- {{reqest_calls}} discovery calls held, both booked by cold email (as of {{reqest_calls:date}}).
- Requests that have gone through the system: {{reqest_requests}} (as of {{reqest_requests:date}}).

## What went wrong
My own research report returned a failing verdict against the criteria I set. As of {{reqest_requests:date}}, no request had gone through the system. I under-invested in distribution and over-invested in the build. What I would do differently is set the supply target before the build target, not after.
